Patterns of the Top 10%

Five things all winning sites do that the bottom half doesn't.

1

Named, Specific Testimonials

TBD Building's testimonials include full names, project addresses, and repeat-client mentions. "R. & J. Whitmore, S. Florida" converts better than "Satisfied Client." The specificity signals authenticity.

2

Founder-First Personal Brand

The highest-converting sites lead with the founder's name, face, and story. "Zach Carpenter" and "Thuraya Ghanem" are the product. Michael already does this — it's a competitive advantage to double down on.

3

Clear 3-Phase Process Framework

RWB (Design/Build/Live), Michael (Vision/Execution/Completion), Kachay (Plan/Build/Lead) — all top sites use a numbered process to reduce client anxiety and demonstrate systematic thinking.

4

Qualification-First Contact

The best sites gate by budget and project type before showing a contact form. This pre-qualifies leads and signals premium positioning. Michael's discovery form already does this — it's a genuine differentiator.

5

Geographic + Niche Specificity

Top performers own a specific geography + niche: "South Florida luxury coastal," "LA architectural masterpieces." Michael's "California to Caribbean" is broad — tightening to "hospitality + ultra-luxury residential" would sharpen positioning and SEO.

Recommended Design Direction

Data-backed recommendations for the next iteration of mcdesign.bio.

Color Palette

Keep #050505 background + gold accent #C9A84C. Add warm stone #E8E0D0 for light sections. The dark/light rhythm creates visual breathing room.

Typography

Inter (current) is correct for body. Add Cormorant Garamond or Playfair Display for display headings only. The serif/sans contrast signals luxury without sacrificing legibility.

Photography

Full-bleed project photography is the #1 gap vs. competitors. Every project needs a hero image. The scroll-sequence canvas is a differentiator — pair it with real project photography in the portfolio grid.

Animation

The scroll-sequence canvas is a genuine differentiator — no competitor has anything close. Keep and enhance. Add subtle parallax on project cards and staggered fade-ins on section entry.

Social Proof

Add named testimonials with project specifics (like TBD Building). Add a "repeat clients" signal. Consider a client logo strip for any commercial/hospitality clients.

Hospitality Positioning

Create a dedicated hospitality section. The SE Texas resort pool (189,000 gal, 18 months) is a standout project that's currently buried. Lead with it for hospitality-focused prospects.
